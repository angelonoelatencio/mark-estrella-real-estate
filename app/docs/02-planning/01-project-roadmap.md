# Project Roadmap

## Overview

This roadmap outlines the planned phases for the development of the **Mark Estrella Real Estate Website**.

The objective is to deliver a scalable, modern, and professional website that supports future business growth.

---

# Phase 1 - MVP (Minimum Viable Product)

**Estimated Duration:** 4 Weeks

### Objectives

* Establish an online presence
* Showcase current property listings
* Generate customer inquiries
* Deploy to production

### Deliverables

* Responsive Website
* Professional Portfolio
* Storyline Section
* Company Information
* Achievements
* Property Listings
* Dynamic Property Details
* Contact Form
* Facebook Messenger Integration
* Domain & Hosting Setup
* Basic SEO

---

# Phase 2 - Business Growth

Estimated Timeline

After MVP Completion

### Deliverables

* Admin Dashboard
* Property Management
* Inquiry Management
* Blog
* Testimonials
* Analytics Dashboard

---

# Phase 3 - Automation

### Deliverables

* AI Chatbot
* CRM Integration
* Appointment Booking
* Email Automation
* Property Recommendation
* Mortgage Calculator

---

# Long-term Vision

The website will evolve from a marketing website into a complete real estate platform capable of managing properties and customer interactions.
