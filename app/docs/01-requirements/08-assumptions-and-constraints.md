# Assumptions and Constraints

## Assumptions

* Client will provide all content and property images.
* Client will purchase the domain and hosting (or approve associated costs).
* Facebook Messenger page is available for integration.
* Property information will initially be managed manually (no admin panel in Phase 1).

## Constraints

* Phase 1 focuses on a public marketing website.
* No authentication or user accounts.
* No online payment processing.
* No property reservation system.
* Admin dashboard is deferred to a future phase.
* AI chatbot is planned as a future enhancement.
