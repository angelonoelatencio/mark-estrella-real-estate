# Project Overview

## Introduction

The Mark Estrella Real Estate Website is a professional website designed to establish an online presence for Mark Estrella as a Real Estate Property Specialist.

The website will serve as a digital portfolio, marketing platform, and lead generation tool.

---

## Objectives

* Showcase professional profile
* Increase online credibility
* Showcase property projects
* Generate customer inquiries
* Increase sales opportunities
* Support future business growth

---

## Target Audience

* Home Buyers
* Investors
* Property Sellers
* OFWs
* Families
* Business Owners
* Property Investors

---

## Success Criteria

* Responsive website
* Professional branding
* Easy navigation
* Fast loading
* Mobile friendly
* Public deployment
* Messenger integration
* Dynamic property pages
