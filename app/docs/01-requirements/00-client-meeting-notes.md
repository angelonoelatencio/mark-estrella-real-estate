# Client Meeting Notes

## Client

Mark Estrella

## Business

Real Estate Property Specialist

---

## Initial Requirements

### Professional Profile

The client wants visitors to know who he is.

Include:

* Professional Profile
* Biography
* Experience
* Contact Information

---

### Company Information

Display the company that the client is affiliated with.

Include:

* Company Logo
* Company Description
* Company Services

---

### Achievements

Display achievements, certifications and awards.

Purpose:

Increase customer trust and credibility.

---

### Property Listings

Current Projects

* Property Project 1
* Property Project 2

Future

The client wants to continuously add more property projects.

The website should support unlimited future projects.

---

### Chat

The client wants customers to contact him through:

* Website
* Facebook Messenger

Future enhancement:

* AI Chatbot

---

### Storyline

The client wants a section that tells his journey as a Real Estate Property Specialist.

---

### Domain

The website should have:

* Custom Domain
* SSL
* Public Hosting

---

## Development Decisions

The website will be developed as a scalable application instead of a static landing page.

Each property project will have its own dedicated page using dynamic routing.

Future projects can be added without redesigning the application.
